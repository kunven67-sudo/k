    THE REAL PERMANENT FIX

    A USB headset adapter, about $10

    It's its own little sound card. Your headphones plug into it, it plugs into a USB port, and the old Realtek driver isn't used at all, so the freezing and the "mic not detected" stuff stop.

    • What to get: a USB to 3.5mm headset adapter with mic (one hole for a 4-ring headset plug, or two holes: green + pink).
    • Plug it into a back USB port on the PC.
    • SOUNDGUARD sees it and switches your sound and mic to it by itself.
    WHAT I DO BY MYSELF

    Live fixes, no restarts

    • Unmute your sound or mic if something muted it
    • Turn volume back up if it hit zero
    • Plug in headphones = sound + calls go to them
    • Calls going to the TV? Moved to your headphones
    • Stereo Mix picked as mic? Switched to your real mic
    • Mic not detected? I tell you to re-plug it
    • Sound frozen? I restart the sound service (2 sec blip)
    • Driver stuck for real? I ask you to restart when you're done. Never by myself.
