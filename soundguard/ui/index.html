        Your sound driver

        –

          USB headset adapter

          The real permanent fix, about $10
          Not plugged in

          It plugs into a USB port and has its own sound chip, so your headphones and mic skip the old Realtek chip completely. Plug it in and SoundGuard switches sound and mic to it by itself.

          1. Search "USB to 3.5mm headset adapter".
          2. Headphones with 1 plug (mic on the same plug): get one with a single headset (TRRS) hole.
          3. Headphones with 2 plugs (green + pink): get one with 2 holes.
          4. Plug it into a back USB port on the PC, plug your headphones into it. Done.

          What I do by myself

          Live fixes, no restarts
          • Unmute your headphones and mic when something mutes them
          • Put volume back when it drops to 0, and keep your mic at your level
          • Keep your headphones and mic as the device for games, apps and Discord calls
          • Turn them back on if Windows turns them off
          • Start Windows Audio if it stops, and restart it (2 second blip) if sound gets stuck
          • Allow the mic again if Windows privacy blocks it
          • If the driver itself is stuck: tell you to restart when you're done. I never restart your PC or touch your games.
