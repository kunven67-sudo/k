REAL LIFE - first playable
==========================

HOW TO PLAY (Windows)
  1. Unzip the whole folder somewhere (Desktop is fine).
  2. Double-click  "Play REAL LIFE.bat"
     A small black window opens (that's the game's mini server) and the game opens in your browser.
     Keep the black window open while you play. Close it when you're done.
  (Browsers won't run a 3D game straight from a folder, that's why the .bat is needed.)

  Other way: drag the whole folder onto Netlify Drop (like Game System) and play from the link.

CONTROLS
  WASD walk, Shift run, Space jump, C crouch, mouse look
  E  use / talk / open / pick up        F  second action (shown on screen)
  Tab  your phone                       Esc  pause / close
  Hold Enter to skip the birth scene

TALKING
  Walk up to anyone and press E, then type anything.
  Settings > People > Talking brain: built-in (works offline), your own Claude API key,
  or a free local AI (Ollama).

Saves itself. One save per life: dying is forever.
