Controls (H hides this)
  • Click the game to look around with the mouse · Esc frees the mouse
  • W A S D walk · Shift run · Ctrl/C crouch · Space jump
  • V first / third person · F2 graphics Low/Medium/High/Ultra · F3 FPS
  • 1 shrink ray: hold left click to charge, let go to fire
  • 2 jar: click on a tiny person to catch them, click at the terrarium to drop them in
  • 3 supplies: click at the terrarium to pour poles, stones, bread or tools in · R changes what
  • Tiny yourself: Click punches · brave tiny people will fight you, scared ones run
  • Look at a tiny person to see how they're doing (they need food and the pond, and build with what you give them)
  • F at the terrarium: shrink yourself in (F again to grow back)
  • Size watch: hold Z to shrink, X to grow (anywhere there's room)
  • 4 hand: click someone small to pick them up · click to put down · right-click throws
  • T / Enter talk: type something to whoever you're looking at (or holding)
  • Shrink ray: left shrinks, right click grows: people (tiny ones even tinier, normal ones into giants), furniture and things, and you (aim at your feet)
  • Mom and Dad give you chores (top right) for money · don't let them see you use the size watch!
  • Shops across the street: E at the door to buy (or steal…) · Tab backpack: eat, drink, use
  • Look under your bed: there's a mouse hole. Shrink below 4 cm to visit the village inside the wall
  • Shrink under 2 cm to see the germ world (dust mites!)
  • P phone: messages, map, weather, bank, wanted · F5 save · F9 load (it also saves by itself)
  • Odd jobs for money: parcels and shifts at the Grocery, lost rings (your phone tells you)
  • F10 settings (graphics, volume, difficulty, and fun not-realistic tiny-world switches)
  • Being tiny is dangerous: eat crumbs, drink droplets, stay warm, dodge feet, bugs, pets and Mom's vacuum. Below 2 cm your body slowly gives out
  • E at your bed: sleep until morning (refills energy)
  • E use: climb the ladder, drink water
  • Step on tiny people to squish them (as a giant, normal people too)
  • People who see you do bad things call the police ★ — get out of sight (or shrink!) until it cools down
