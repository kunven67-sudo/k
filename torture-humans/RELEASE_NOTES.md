## Torture Humans 0.1.0 (early test version)

Not the finished game: this is what's built so far, so you can try it.

- Your basement lab: terrarium with a tiny world inside (hills, pond, lava, rocks, logs, ferns), bug farm, clone tube, surgery table, specimen jars
- Climb the metal ladder (E) up through the hatch into your bedroom
- Shrink ray (1): hold to charge, release to fire. Jar (2): catch tiny people, drop them in the terrarium
- F at the terrarium: shrink yourself inside; F again to grow back
- Step on tiny people; gore setting decides what you see
- Health, hunger, thirst, energy; lava burns, pond water to drink
- 3 visitors walk around the lab (the town comes later)

First start downloads the game files once (about 1.4 GB), then it starts in seconds.
