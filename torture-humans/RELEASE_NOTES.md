## Torture Humans 0.2.0 (early test version)

Not the finished game yet: this is what's built so far.

### Runs much faster
- The game picks graphics settings for your graphics card on first start (a GT 1030 or laptop graphics gets Low)
- **F2** changes graphics (Low / Medium / High / Ultra), **F3** shows FPS
- Low draws about 7x less per frame than before: fewer shadow redraws, cheaper glass, small lamps off
- Laptops use the real graphics card instead of the built-in one

### Tiny people live in the terrarium
- They get hungry and thirsty (and die if you don't feed them), drink at the pond, eat bread crumbs
- **3 = Supplies**: pour in poles, stones, bread, a tiny axe or a tiny pickaxe where you aim (R changes it)
- With the axe they chop wood off logs, with the pickaxe they break stone off rocks
- They build a campfire, then a lean-to, a tipi and a stone hut, piece by piece
- Look at one to see what they're doing and how they feel
- Shrink yourself in (F): brave ones fight you, scared ones run. Click to punch back

### Fixes
- Aiming into the terrarium goes through the glass (the jar drops people where you point, and catches people inside)
- Catching someone who was already in the terrarium no longer crashes

First start downloads the game files once (about 1.4 GB), then it starts in seconds.
