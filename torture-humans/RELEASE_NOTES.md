## Torture Humans 0.4.0 (early test version)

Not the finished game yet: this is what's built so far. New since 0.3.0:

### Faces
- Wrinkles that only show with the expression: forehead lines, frown lines, crow's feet
- Tears run down the cheeks when someone is very sad or terrified
- Bruises where people get punched: purple, then green, then yellow, then gone

### Hair, skin, clothes
- Hair sways in the wind and with sudden moves; wet hair in the rain
- Outdoor workers are tanned; old clothes are faded at the knees and elbows
- Ultra: soft-body jiggle (chest and belly)

### Weather
- Snow below freezing: flakes, white roofs and ground, melting when it warms up
- High+: sunlight shafts through the bedroom window

### The terrarium
- Bugs: ants marching between their nest and food, beetles, a spider (tiny people run from it)
- Farming: Supplies (3) now has seeds; tiny people plant them, crops grow berries, they pick and eat them

Everything from 0.3.0 is still here (town, day/night, rain, the tiny colony...). F2 graphics, F3 FPS.
