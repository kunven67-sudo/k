# 🌀 RIFT LAB: THE MASTER LIST

Everything that goes in the game. Made from every answer you gave (two chats), plus real life.

### 📖 How to read this list
- ✅ = **you picked it**
- 🧠 = **Claude picked it** (your rule: "whatever is realistic / depends on everything")
- ⚙️ = **it's a setting** (you can change it)
- 🏷️ = **made-up name**, because real brands, movies, shows, consoles, graphics cards etc. can't legally be copied. They look and work like the real thing, just with a different name.
- ⭐ = **gets built FIRST** (Empty World + spawn menu, Furniture tab first)

### 🤝 Honest notes (no lies)
- Real life has millions of things, so no list can have *literally* everything. This one has the **main real stuff**, and it keeps growing every time you say "add ___".
- This list is the **plan**, not the finished game. Building all of it takes a LONG time. It gets built **piece by piece**, and every piece gets tested (collisions, animations, looks) before I call it done.
- The game runs in your browser (a link you click to play) on your gaming PC. It'll look as good as I can make a browser game look. Honestly, that's not GTA 6 level, but it's still going to look good.

## 1. 🎬 MAIN MENU, MAPS + STARTING A WORLD

### 1.1 First launch
- ✅ Short intro + **skip button**: late night in your garage, you finish building **The Rift Tool**, test it, something shrinks 😳, then the character maker
- ✅ **First-time tips**: a small hint the first time you do something ("hold + drag to open"), then never again ⚙️ (can be turned off)

### 1.2 Main menu
- ✅ **RIFT LAB logo**: torn letters with a rift glowing through the crack, light bending around the edges, slow flicker + hum
- ✅ **Live 3D background**: a slow camera from space → Earth → down through the clouds → into the forest → shrinks into a **dewdrop on a leaf** 💧
- ✅ **Clean see-through glass menus** over the background
- Buttons: Continue · New World · Load World (many save slots) · Character Maker · Settings · Achievements · Credits · Quit
- 🧠 Menu music: soft and spacey, changes as the camera goes from space down to the dewdrop
- 🧠 Hover sounds + smooth animated menu transitions (panels slide/fade, nothing just "pops")

### 1.3 The 3 maps
1. ⭐ **Empty World** (sandbox 1): untouched nature, spawn anything
2. ✅ **Mansion Town** (sandbox 2): your mansion + a small California coast town (grows into a bigger city later if it runs smooth)
3. ✅ **Portal Lab**: rebuilt later (the old one was lost), with portals, cubes, buttons, doors, gel, turrets + seeing yourself in portals

### 1.4 New-world menu (choose when making a world)
- ✅ **Map**: Empty World / Mansion Town / Portal Lab
- ✅ **Terrain** (Empty World): ⭐ **Grass meadow + oak/maple forest** (first + most detailed), Desert + canyon, Snow + mountains, Beach + ocean, White grid (test world)
- ✅ **Map type**: new each world, made like real geology (rivers carve valleys downhill into lakes) + a **seed number** you can share
- ✅ **Start date + time**: today's real date + time from your PC (sun, moon phase, stars + season all match)
- ✅ **Day length**: real 24 h / 2 h / 48 min / 24 min per day (fast-forward x10 / x100 / x1000 works on top)
- ✅ **Spawn mode**: Free (spawn anything, still shows real prices) OR Pay (costs money)
- ✅ **How spawned stuff arrives**: appears where you point OR delivery truck/drone drives it out
- ✅ **Character**: use your main character OR make a new one for this world
- ✅ **Start kit** (Empty World): just clothes + phone (survival) 🧠 plus kit options ⚙️: nothing / camping kit / full survival kit
- ✅ **Household** (Mansion): alone / staff / family / staff + family
- ✅ **Starting money** (Mansion): rich inventor (you sold inventions, own the mansion, still pay bills, taxes, staff, power)
- ✅ **Natural disasters**: off / real-life chance / more often, plus trigger them yourself
- ✅ **Crime**: real, depending on the person ⚙️ (off / low / real)
- ✅ **Death mode**: hospital + bill / respawn at bed / permadeath (world ends, saved as a memory)
- ✅ **God mode**: on/off (no needs, no damage)
- ✅ **Gore**: OFF by default
- ✅ **Gun battery**: real battery / self-recharge / unlimited
- 🧠 **World name** + thumbnail picture taken automatically

### 1.5 Saving
- ✅ **Many save slots** + 🧠 autosave every few minutes ⚙️ + quick save
- ✅ **Save + load** everything you spawned, built, broke, dirtied, and every germ's history

## 3. 🧍 YOU (THE PLAYER)

### 3.1 Your body
- ✅ **Full real body**: stamina, heavy breathing, sweat, limping, bleeding, **broken bones that heal slowly**, bruises, cuts, scars that stay
- ✅ **Real strength**: light stuff you carry, heavy stuff you push/drag slowly, a piano needs help, a dolly or a forklift
- ✅ **Real temperature**: cold → shiver → hypothermia, heat → sweat → heatstroke, sunburn, wet clothes make you colder, jackets, campfires warm you
- ✅ **Needs**: food, water, sleep, bathroom ✅🧠 (god mode turns them off)
- 🧠 Illness: you can catch colds, flu, food poisoning from germs (washing hands helps!)
- 🧠 Hygiene: you get dirty, sweaty, smelly (people notice 😂), shower/bath to clean
- 🧠 Your skills get better with practice (cooking, building, driving, shooting, swimming, fishing, electronics...)
- 🧠 Weight changes slowly with food + exercise

### 3.2 How you feel it (no HUD)
- ✅ **No HUD by default** (⚙️ HUD on/off setting)

### 3.4 Using things
