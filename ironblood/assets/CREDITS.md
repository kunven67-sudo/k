# Asset credits

All 3D scans and textures in this folder come from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) and are **CC0** (public domain).
They were reduced for real-time use (fewer triangles, leaf cards thinned and enlarged, color + alpha merged into PNG).

fir_tree_01, tree_small_02 (as oak.glb), dead_tree_trunk, dead_tree_trunk_02, tree_stump_01, root_cluster_01,
dry_branches_medium_01, fern_02, grass_medium_01, grass_medium_02, moss_01, rock_moss_set_01, rock_moss_set_02,
namaqualand_boulder_02, stone_fire_pit, wooden_crate_01, kite_shield, large_castle_door.
Ground/building textures and the HDRI sky are loaded straight from Poly Haven at runtime.

faces.glb: villager and player faces built from the MakeHuman base mesh, face-shape targets and high-poly eyes
(MakeHuman, **CC0**, makehumancommunity.org). Skin, lips, brows and stubble are painted by IRONBLOOD's own baker.
