# Chill Charge

Android app that helps your phone charge faster and last longer, with lo-fi music and games powered by your real charging.

- **ONE button (SAVE POWER)**: brightness 20%, screen sleeps after 15 s, auto-rotate off, touch vibration + sounds off,
  app animations off. Works plugged in (charges faster) and unplugged (lasts longer). Tap again = everything back.
  Battery Saver: Android doesn't let apps flip it, so the app points you to it.
- **Real watts** from the battery mAh counter, measured between the moments it changes (some phones only update it
  every 30+ s) and held between updates. Unplugged it shows USING watts + time left.
- **GAMES** (more real watts = stronger): Wattbot Arena (robot boss fights), Volt Rush (auto races, 6 leagues),
  Terra (terraform a spinning planet), Power Empire (idle builder with Power Bank + Research).
- **Extras**: PC link (play + watch on your PC over Wi-Fi, port 8080 + key), Claude mode, send phone info.

Build: `bash build.sh` (see the top of the file for the SDK download).

Music (CC0, OpenGameArt.org "Relaxing Lo-Fi Songs" + "Since 2 A.M."). Font: Space Grotesk (SIL OFL).
