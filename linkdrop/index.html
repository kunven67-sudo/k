    How it works

    1. In TikTok tap Share, then Copy link.
    2. Come back here and tap PASTE. Copied a bunch? Paste them all in the box at once.
    3. It downloads and saves to your phone. Everything you got shows in History.
    4. If your phone asks "Download multiple files?", tap Allow so a bunch of links can save in one go.
