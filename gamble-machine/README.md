# Gamble Machine

A single-file browser game: work jobs, hit the bar, drive your car, and blow it all at the casino.
Open `index.html` in any modern browser (desktop or phone). No install, no build step.

## What's in it
- **Casino with 5 slot machines**, each with its own reel strips and paytable, tuned to real-casino returns (92–98% RTP):
  Lucky Cherry (classic 3-reel), Diamond Rush (wild diamonds), Pharaoh's Fortune (5 reels, 5 lines),
  Hot Sevens (multiplier reel), and Mega Jackpot (progressive jackpot that grows with every bet).
- **Jobs**: flip burgers at Burger Barn, stack steel beams with a swinging crane at the construction site,
  and deliver pizza for Tony against the clock.
- **Your car**: suspension bounce, momentum, your guy's head bobbling in the window. Speed or drive drunk and the cops show up.
- **The bar**: real-ish BAC. Drinks absorb over time and wear off slowly. Over 0.08 jobs send you home and police
  arrest you for DUI. Past 0.30 you black out and wake up with less money.
- **Jiggle physics**: every character has spring-driven head, cheeks, hair and belly that wobble when they walk,
  jump, bump into each other or get bounced around in the car.
- Day/night cycle, synthesized sound, and auto-save in the browser.

## Controls
| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| A / D or arrows | Walk (hold to run, or Shift) |
| Space / W | Jump (spins on a slot machine) |
| E / Enter | Doors, machines, bar, knock |
| F | Get in / out of the car |
| H | Honk |
| 1–4 | Flip patties on the burger job |
| M | Sound on/off |

On phones, on-screen buttons appear automatically.
