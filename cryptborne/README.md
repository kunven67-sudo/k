# Cryptborne

A pixel-art dungeon crawler in a single HTML file. It needs no install and no extra files: open `index.html` in a browser.

## How to play

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| WASD / arrows | Move |
| Left click | Attack toward the mouse (hold to keep swinging) |
| C / Space | Dodge roll (you can't be hit while rolling) |
| F (hold) | Block. Needs a shield. Block right before a hit to **parry** and stun the monster. |
| E | Talk, shop, open chests, enter dungeons, use portals |
| Q | Drink your best health potion |
| 1-0 | Use an inventory bar slot |
| M or click the tiny map | World map: pick a dungeon and travel there |
| B / I | Inventory bag |
| Esc | Pause, save, settings, quit to menu |

Phones and tablets get an on-screen joystick and buttons.

## What's inside

- **Main menu** with Play and Settings. Saves show when each one was created, last loaded and last saved.
- **Emberfall**, a town with people who walk around, Mara's General Store (potions, buys your loot), Brom's Forge (weapons, shields, armor) and a healing fountain.
- **Six dungeons**, each locked behind a level. Higher dungeons have tougher monsters and better chests:

| Dungeon | Level | Monsters | Boss |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mossy Hollow | 1 | Slime, Cave Bat | Slime King |
| Goblin Warrens | 3 | Goblin, Cave Bat, Slime | Goblin Chief |
| Bonecrypt | 5 | Skeleton, Skeleton Archer, Cave Bat | Skeleton Knight |
| Webspire Depths | 8 | Giant Spider, Skeleton, Skeleton Archer | Spider Queen |
| Ashen Forge | 11 | Fire Imp, Orc Brute, Stone Golem (and lava) | Magma Golem |
| Wraithmoor Tomb | 15 | Wraith, Stone Golem, Skeletons | Lich |

- Every dungeon layout is random each visit. Bosses guard a golden chest and the portal home.
- Each monster type drops its own loot (Slime Gel, Bat Wing, Goblin Ear, Bone, Skull, Spider Silk, Ectoplasm and more) that you sell for coins.
- Weapons include swords, axes, a war hammer, bows (one freezes enemies), a fireball staff, a poison dagger and a lifesteal scythe.
