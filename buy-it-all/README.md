# Buy It All

A browser life sim about earning money and buying real-life stuff.

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step and no internet connection needed. Progress saves in your browser.

- **Shops:** food and drinks, PC parts (CPUs, graphics cards, RAM, power supplies and more), electronics, furniture, vehicles from skateboards to private jets, clothes, luxury goods, pets and homes.
- **Survival:** keep food, drink, energy, fun and health up. Sleep every night. Rent and bills are due every 7 days.
- **Career:** start at fast food and work up to CEO. Jobs need things you buy, like a bike, a suit, a car or a strong computer.
- **PC builder:** install parts into slots, keep the power supply big enough, then game, freelance, stream or mine crypto.
- **Money:** savings, loans and a stock market with daily price moves.
- **Goal:** collect every item and unlock all the achievements.
