# Portal Gun — Part 1

An unofficial Rick and Morty fan game. You play as Rick: wake Morty up, drag him to the garage, fly the Space Cruiser, and mess around the Smith house with the portal gun.

## How to play
Download `index.html` and open it in Chrome or Edge. It's one file and works offline. Edge has the best text-to-speech voices.

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Mouse | Look |
| Left click | Shoot a portal |
| W A S D | Walk (Shift to run, Space to jump) |
| E | Use / talk |
| Wheel, Q, or 1–6 | Turn the dial on the gun |
| R | Close your portals |
| V | Say a catchphrase |
| B / F | Burp / drink from the flask |
| Esc | Pause and settings |

In the Space Cruiser: the mouse steers, W/S is the throttle, A/D strafe, Space/C go up and down, Shift boosts, and E gets you out.

## What's in Part 1
- The pilot-style night intro: wake Morty, drag him to the garage, fly, and the neutrino bomb rant
- The whole Smith house, the garage, and the town
- The portal gun, with portal fluid you have to refill
- Morty, Jerry, Beth, Summer, the Butter Robot, and Mr. Poopybutthole
- Interdimensional Cable on the living-room TV

## Honest notes
- The voices are your browser's text-to-speech, not the real actors.
- The characters are my own drawings in the show's style.
- The music is original. Use **Settings → Play my own music** for any song file on your computer.
- The TV bits are new jokes in the style of the show, not copies of the episodes.

## Building from source
```
npm install
npm run build   # bundles src/ into index.html
```
