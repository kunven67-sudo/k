# LATE NIGHT CRUNCH

A cozy late-night clicker. Your guy is chilling on the couch in a rainy room at 2 AM, eating chips. Every bite earns coins, and coins buy better chips. The better the chip, the crazier it looks: real flavors at first, then lava, frost, gold, lightning, galaxy, rainbow dragon, and finally a black hole chip that wants to eat your room.

Everything (art, music, sounds) is drawn and synthesized in code. It's one HTML file with no downloads.

## Play

Open `late-night-crunch.html` in a browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari). Works on PC and phones.

- **Tap / click anywhere in the room** (or press **Space**) to make him eat faster.
- **Mash fast** to fill the **CRUNCH FRENZY** meter: 10 seconds of 3x coins, both hands, glowing eyes, harder music.
- **Drag** the room (or use the arrow keys / mouse wheel) to look around on small screens.

## What's in it

- **16 chips**: Plain, Sea Salt, BBQ, Sour Cream & Onion, Nacho Cheese, Flamin' Hot, Lava, Frost, Toxic, Gold, Diamond, Lightning, Glitch, Galaxy, Rainbow Dragon, Black Hole. Each has its own look, bag, crunch sound, reaction from your guy, and room vibe.
- **11 snack buddies** who eat with you and earn coins on their own, even while the game is closed: Dog, Cat, Hamster, Robot Vacuum, Parrot, Gamer Friend, Sleepy Friend, Bag Goblin, Big Friend, Ghost, Alien. Level them up (2x boosts at Lv 25, 50, 100, 200).
- **13 room upgrades** that show up in the room and boost all coins: posters, fairy lights, plants, lava lamp, comfy couch, mini fridge, neon sign, big TV, gaming PC, fish tank, RGB LED strips, disco ball, gamer couch.
- **Dips** (permanent multipliers) and **drinks** (boosts with cooldowns): cola, energy drink, milkshake, midnight coffee.
- **Rebirth**: let the Black Hole chip swallow the room, wake up with a permanent coin multiplier, plus new room themes (Arcade Basement, Treehouse, Beach Hut, Space Station), outfits (glowing headphones, flame hoodie, gold chain, galaxy hoodie, crown) and shiny chips.
- **32 trophies**, each worth +2% coins forever.
- **Lofi music** that adds instruments as you unlock better chips, and real late-night room sounds (rain on the window, cars, a far-off siren, the fridge humming, PC fans, the TV, the clock ticking).

## Saving

The game saves by itself in your browser. Settings has **Download save** / **Load save** to back it up or move it to another device. Your buddies keep earning for up to 12 hours while you're away.

## Settings

Music / sound / room-sound volume, graphics (Auto, Low, Medium, High), screen shake, and vibration. Auto graphics lowers the quality by itself if the game starts dropping frames.
