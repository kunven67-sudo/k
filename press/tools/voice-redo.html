HOST's lines. Record each one, listen back, redo as many times as you want, pick your best take (green), then Save.
How to sound good: sit close to the mic (a hand away), quiet room, no fan. HOST is calm and polite at first, like a hotel worker who's a little too nice. Talk slower than normal, leave little pauses at the "..." parts. Don't act scary early on, the creepy comes from him being too calm. Your voice gets changed into a robot voice after, so don't worry how your real voice sounds. Press Record, wait half a second, then talk.
