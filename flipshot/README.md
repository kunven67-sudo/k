# FLIPSHOT

Flip a bottle. Better bottles fly higher: off the kitchen table, over the city, past the clouds, into space, and way past that. Then things get weird.

Open `flipshot.html` in a browser on PC or phone. It's one file with nothing to install. Your progress saves in the browser on its own, and you can download a save file from Menu > Save to keep it safe or move it to another device.

`flipshot-trailer.mp4` is a recorded run through the whole game with the game's own sound. It shows everything, so skip it if you don't want spoilers.

`flipshot-holidays.mp4` shows what the game looks like on Halloween, Christmas, New Year and Valentine's Day.

## Controls

- Space, Enter or tap: flip. Landing is luck: better bottles and upgrades raise your odds.
- Space (or V, or tap FLIP) while falling: open your parachute once you buy one in Upgrades. You float down slowly, even with Fall Speed maxed.
- B bottles, U upgrades, M missions, P places, D daily, S style, K book, T trophies, O menu
- A auto flip on/off, R replay your last flip
- Esc closes menus, Tab switches menu tabs, arrow keys move through menu buttons

More keys show up when you need them. Every key can be changed in Menu > Keys.
