# TINY TANK

A glass tank on a garage workbench, full of tiny people (1:100 scale, about 1.8 cm tall).
Spawn them from the spawn menu, keep them fed and watered, and watch them live:
they build huts out of Lego bricks and cardboard, light campfires with matches, make LED lamps,
plant seeds, fall in love, get married, have babies, fight, grieve and bury their dead.

Open `tiny-tank.html` in a desktop browser (PC, mouse + keyboard). Internet is needed the first time
to load three.js from the jsDelivr CDN.

## Controls
- Right-drag: spin the camera. Middle-drag or WASD: move. Mouse wheel: zoom.
- 1 Hand (click = info, drag = pick up, Shift = lift high), 2 Flick, 3 Squish, 4 Medicine,
  5 Tweezers, 6 Paint floor, 7 Mist sprayer, T Talk
- Tab: spawn menu. Space: pause. , and . : speed. L: cage lamp. H: heat lamp. C: center. F: follow selected. Esc: menu.

## Notes
- Kids and babies can never be hurt by anything.
- Optional: paste your own Claude API key in Settings so the people understand everything you say
  and talk to each other with AI (costs API money, has a daily limit).
- Saves: 5 slots in the browser, auto-save every minute, plus download/upload save files.
